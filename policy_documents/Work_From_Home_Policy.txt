WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Employees may work from home subject to company policy and manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their normal working hours.

Employees must attend important meetings and business activities as required.

Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure working environment while working remotely.

Company confidential information must not be exposed to unauthorized individuals while working from home.

Work from home requests may be restricted based on business requirements, employee role, project requirements, or operational needs.
