LEAVE POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Casual Leave

Employees are eligible for 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year.

Casual Leave may be used for personal requirements and short-term absence.

Employees should apply for Casual Leave in advance whenever possible.

2. Sick Leave

Employees are eligible for 12 days of Sick Leave per calendar year.

Sick Leave may be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness.

The company may request supporting medical documentation for extended periods of absence.

3. Earned Leave

Employees are eligible for 24 days of Earned Leave per calendar year.

Earned Leave should normally be requested in advance and is subject to manager approval.

4. Leave Approval

Leave requests are subject to manager approval.

Employees should ensure that their leave does not negatively impact critical business activities.

5. Leave Balance

Employees can check their current leave balances through the HR system.

Unused leave may be carried forward only according to applicable company rules.
